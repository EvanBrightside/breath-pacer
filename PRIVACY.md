# Privacy Policy — Breath Pacer

Last updated: 2026-08-19

Breath Pacer is a standalone Zepp OS mini app. It runs entirely on the watch.

## Not a medical device

Breath Pacer is a relaxation and mindfulness aid. It is not a medical device, it does
not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, and the heart rate figures it
shows are not a clinical measurement. Do not use it as a substitute for medical
advice.

## What the app accesses

- **Heart rate.** With your permission, the app switches the watch's heart rate
  sensor into continuous measurement mode for the duration of a breathing
  session, so it can show you your heart rate at the start and end of that
  session. Measurement stops when you leave the session screen.
- **Local storage on the watch.** The app stores your chosen technique, your
  chosen session length, and a count of completed sessions and total minutes.

## What the app does with it

**Breath Pacer itself transmits nothing.** It has no network access, no phone
companion component, no analytics, and no advertising.

One thing is worth stating plainly rather than leaving implied: because the app
switches the sensor into continuous measurement mode, the readings taken during
a session are recorded by the watch's own health system. That system is operated
by Zepp, not by this app, and it may sync those readings to the Zepp app and
Zepp's services the same way it does for heart rate data recorded by any other
activity on the watch. That syncing is governed by Zepp's own privacy policy.

Breath Pacer does not store heart rate values anywhere itself. They are displayed on
screen and discarded when the session ends. The only things written to the app's
storage are your technique preference, your session-length preference, and two
counters.

## Sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no
means to do so.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app from the watch erases everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.
